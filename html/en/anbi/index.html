Purpose

Conducting liturgical services according to the Russian Orthodox tradition; administering the Sacraments; providing pastoral and diaconal assistance to Orthodox believers.

The construction and maintenance of church buildings and/or premises for worship of the Russian Orthodox Church in the city of Rotterdam (Schiedamsesingel 220 and Persijnstraat 16), as well as for the cultural and social activities of that Church, and everything connected with and conducive to the foregoing.
Policy plan

As a community, they care for the needs of their church, their clergy and their parish with all its institutions, as well as for the general needs of the diocese. Furthermore, the Parish aims to build a Christian life together in loving unity of prayer and worship, through liturgical celebrations and sacraments.

The Sacraments — such as baptism, chrismation (Myron anointing), marriage, confession, communion and the anointing of the sick — are the visible work of the clergy, through which God’s grace wondrously conveys gifts to the believers who receive them. Individual believers are also supported through conversations with the clergy.
